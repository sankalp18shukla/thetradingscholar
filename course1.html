FAQs

Common questions answered

Is this suitable for beginners?
The course is designed to help traders understand algorithmic trading from foundational concepts through to live deployment. Prior coding knowledge is helpful but not required — the curriculum is structured to build competence progressively.
Do I need coding knowledge to enroll?
The course focuses on helping traders build automated systems without requiring extensive technical expertise. The emphasis is on understanding logic and structure, not becoming a software engineer.
What if I miss a live session?
All live sessions are recorded and made available in your student portal. Recordings remain accessible throughout the full duration of your program so you can review any session at your own pace.
Is there ongoing support after the course ends?
Support and continued guidance are included depending on the program level selected. The highest tier includes live trading supervision, ongoing mentorship during deployment, and ongoing access to Q&A sessions.
