02 / Personal Narrative Origins • Movement • Focus

story & life.

I grew up in Korba — yeah, you've probably never heard of it. I've lived across five-plus cities since. I studied psychology (honours), mostly because it was fun.

I never wanted a conventional career, so I joined a startup that was barely six months old and learned everything by doing it. Right now I'm an Early MBA programme student at INSEAD. When I'm not working, I'm lifting, surfing, or doing any sport that'll have me. I also make content over at @ftlogaditi.

Different city, different chapter — same person chasing whatever's fun.

03 / Initiatives Social Impact & Ventures

initiatives.

Some of the stuff I'm proudest of never made me a rupee. I co-founded The Pet Story after losing my own pet, and we ran feeding and rescue drives, documented people's stories, and once got a university to answer for a cruelty case.

I tutored kids at ChildrenWise until they made me academic manager. I helped women artisans in Korba sell more one Diwali. I've painted government-school walls so classrooms felt less grey.

I don't have a tidy reason for any of it beyond wanting to help. That's kind of the point.
