Academic Specifications

Program Details & FAQs

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes, we start completely from zero. No prior coding or complex algorithmic knowledge is required to begin learning this program.

What platform / broker is required?

We primarily support Zerodha Kite Connect API and Dhan API. We cover the step-by-step connection process during Module 5.

Do I need coding knowledge?

No prior coding experience is needed. The core Python programmatic concepts required for trading automation are structured directly within our system.

What markets does this cover?

The system is designed and optimized to trade NSE Options, Futures, and Equity market segments.

What if I miss live sessions?

All live lectures are recorded and cataloged in the portal within 24 hours. You can review them anytime at your own convenience.

How long is the course?

The program runs for 8 weeks, introducing 1 comprehensive module per week alongside interactive live weekend review sessions.

Is there ongoing support after the course?

Standard and Premium tiers feature 3-month support. Premium users receive lifetime update access and priority communication channels.

How many sessions are included?

The core mentorship package includes 8 highly comprehensive 1:1 sessions over a 2-month period, combined with consistent daily support over WhatsApp.

Is this for beginners?

We recommend having at least basic market experience. Students should ideally have traded for at least a couple of months and understood standard terminology beforehand.

How do I apply?

You can fill out the application form on our site. Kundan personally reviews every single form submission and invites qualified candidates for a 15-minute discovery call to assess fit.

What markets / instruments are covered?

Based entirely on your personalized profile, capital size, and goals, we structure strategies across NSE Options (weekly/monthly), Futures, and liquid Equities.

Is there a refund policy?

Due to the highly exclusive, time-consuming nature of direct 1:1 personalized mentorship, fees are non-refundable once the sessions have commenced.

What happens if I miss a session?

We completely understand timing issues. Scheduled interactive sessions can be easily rescheduled as long as you notify your mentor at least 24 hours in advance.

Do I need to know options before taking this course?

Basic option literacy is recommended. You must understand what puts and calls are, how buying and selling functions, and what expiry parameters represent. True beginners should read our foundational literature first.

Is option selling risky?

Naked option selling introduces asymmetric risk. Because of this, our program strictly covers hedged execution architectures—ensuring your absolute max risk profile is defined prior to placement.

What markets and instruments are covered?

We specialize heavily in NSE weekly Nifty and BankNifty options, alongside structured monthly capital compounding strategies.

How much capital do I need to start?

We require a minimum capital structure of ₹2L for basic strategies. A balance of ₹5L+ is highly recommended to execute fully optimized Iron Condor margin-hedged setups.

What if I miss live sessions?

No problem at all. All live webinar elements are fully captured, indexed, and available inside the user dashboard within 24 hours of recording.

What platform / broker is needed?

Any major Indian broker with active NSE F&O segment permissions will work. We strongly recommend Zerodha, Dhan, or Angel One for optimized order book execution.
