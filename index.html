What is systematic algorithmic trading?
Systematic trading utilizes rules-based logic to handle entry, exit, and sizing parameters. Automating these protocols completely removes emotional and psychological bias from execution flows, enabling consistent performance.
Do I need specialized infrastructure to run codes?
No. During the execution phase, we utilize hosted virtual private servers (AWS cloud services) so systems run independently without manual interventions or a dedicated machine.
Is this suitable for complete beginners?
Yes. Our programs are structured from foundational market concepts all the way to advanced institutional execution techniques. You do not need prior trading experience to join our beginner-friendly pathways.
How long does it take to see results?
Results vary based on consistency of practice. Most students begin implementing live strategies within 4–8 weeks. Measurable, sustainable performance improvement typically appears in the 2–4 month window for committed learners.
Is there a community or support group?
Yes. All enrolled students gain access to our private community channel where Kundan and senior traders share live market insights, strategy updates, and performance reviews on a regular basis.
What programmatic setups are covered in the modules?
We cover multi-leg option combinations, trend-following momentum models, and institutional-style delta risk mitigation tools utilizing Python and broker APIs.
Are performance P&L statements verified?
Yes. Our student metrics highlight real account execution sheets with defined drawdowns and maximum drawdown risk caps. All showcased results are from live broker account statements.
What brokers are supported in the Elgo system?
The Elgo system currently supports Zerodha (Kite API), Angel One SmartAPI, Upstox API, Alice Blue Ant API, and 5Paisa. Additional broker integrations are added based on student demand.
How often is course content updated?
Course content is reviewed and updated quarterly based on market structure changes, SEBI regulatory updates, and student feedback. Major strategy overhauls are communicated directly through the community channel.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee for the core modules. If you complete the first week of content and do not find value in the methodology, we will process a full refund without conditions.
How does the 1-on-1 model function?
Every session is scheduled directly on a live shared desk with Kundan Prajapati. We construct personalized rule sets customized entirely to your capital size, trading schedule, and long-term financial goals.
Is there active post-session support?
Yes. All alumni receive access to our community forums where ongoing code updates, market structure changes, and structural strategy revisions are shared periodically with detailed commentary.
How many sessions are included in the package?
The standard mentorship package includes 8 one-hour live sessions over 4 weeks. Extended packages of 16 and 24 sessions are also available for traders requiring deeper, long-term structural transformation.
Can I review sessions later if I miss one?
Yes. All live sessions are recorded and made available in your student portal within 24 hours of completion. Recordings remain accessible for the full duration of your mentorship package.
What is the minimum capital required to begin?
There is no fixed minimum capital requirement to enroll. However, for optimal strategy execution, we recommend a minimum of ₹2,00,000 in your trading account to properly implement the hedging systems taught in the program.
