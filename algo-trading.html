The Curriculum

8 Modules to
Complete Automation

01

Introduction to Algorithmic Trading & Institutional Systems

Understand what institutional algo trading looks like vs. retail approaches. Framework thinking, risk culture, and the mindset required for systematic execution.

02

Building Your First Trading Strategy — Logic & Framework

Define entry, exit, and sizing rules. Translate your market ideas into precise, codeable logic that can be backtested and automated.

03

Backtesting — Validating Strategies With Historical Data

Run your strategy against years of market data. Identify edge cases, stress-test assumptions, and verify statistical validity before going live.

04

Risk Management Systems — Position Sizing & Drawdown Control

Kelly Criterion, fixed-fractional sizing, max drawdown rules, and circuit breakers. The mechanics that protect capital at all times.

05

Live Deployment — Connecting Strategy to Broker API

Go live on Zerodha Kite, Angel One SmartAPI, or Upstox. Webhook triggers, order management, and error handling in production environments.

06

Optimization & Scaling — Improving Performance Over Time

Walk-forward analysis, parameter optimization (avoiding overfitting), and scaling capital within the same framework structure.

07

Advanced Hedging Integration Within Algo Systems

Build delta-neutral hedges and protective overlays directly into automated strategy logic for all market condition robustness.

08

Live Trading Supervision & Ongoing Support

Real-time system monitoring, community review sessions, and ongoing strategy refinement with mentor oversight for sustained improvement.

Common Questions

Everything You
Want to Know

Is this suitable for complete beginners with no coding background?

Yes. The Elgo system is designed from the ground up for non-coders. Module 1 starts with complete fundamentals. You will learn enough Python to automate your strategy without needing a computer science degree.

What platforms and brokers are supported?

Zerodha (Kite API), Angel One SmartAPI, Upstox API, Alice Blue Ant API, and 5Paisa are all supported. Additional brokers are added based on student demand.

What markets does this course cover?

The course primarily covers Indian equity and F&O markets (NSE/BSE). Strategies are built for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and individual equity options and futures.

What if I miss live sessions?

All live sessions are recorded and uploaded to your student portal within 24 hours. You can replay them as many times as needed within your access duration.

How long is the course in total?

The core 8 modules span approximately 6–8 weeks of structured learning. Live sessions, Q&As, and the ongoing support run for 3 months on the Standard tier (lifetime on Premium).

Is there ongoing support after the course ends?

Yes. All students have access to the private community channel where Kundan and senior traders share live insights, strategy updates, and performance reviews on an ongoing basis.
